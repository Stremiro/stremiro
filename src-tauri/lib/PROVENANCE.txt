Native runtime pair provenance — status 2026-11-06
=====================================================

The two DLLs in this directory are the packaged libmpv pair
(tauri.conf.json ships lib/*.dll as resources). Assert-NativeRuntimePair in
scripts/build-tauri.ps1 fails packaging if this pair is missing, duplicated,
locked, or diverges from a dev-time copy at src-tauri/.
That packaging consistency check does not authenticate either binary.
Publication remains blocked until the complete runtime is attributable,
licensed for distribution and passes native TLS/origin-isolation checks.
